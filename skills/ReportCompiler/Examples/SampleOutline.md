## Areas to Cover

### 1. Capacity Planning

**Key Questions:**
- How are they forecasting demand?
- What's the accuracy of their forecasts?
- How far out do they plan?

**Findings to Highlight:**
- 38% invisible call volume (IVR attribution gap)
- Forecast accuracy issues during OE
- Limited long-range planning

**Documents to Reference:**
- Masterfile Metric Snapshot (forecasting sheets)
- WFM Process Summary deck
- Call with WFM team lead (12/15)

---

### 2. Real-Time Management

**Key Questions:**
- How do they manage intraday variations?
- What's their response time to queue issues?
- How is adherence managed?

**Findings to Highlight:**
- Reactive rather than proactive
- Limited intraday reforecasting
- Adherence tracking but minimal action

**Documents to Reference:**
- WFM SOPs folder
- RTM supervisor interviews

---

### 3. Technology Landscape

**Key Questions:**
- What systems are in use?
- How well integrated are they?
- What's the automation level?

**Findings to Highlight:**
- Verint/IEX vs Avaya/Genesys fragmentation
- Manual data reconciliation between systems
- Limited automation opportunities identified

**Documents to Reference:**
- CE Strategy and Technology deck
- IVR analysis spreadsheets
- Tech team interviews

---

### 4. Process Maturity

**Key Questions:**
- Are processes documented?
- Is there variance analysis?
- How do learnings flow back to planning?

**Findings to Highlight:**
- SOPs exist but inconsistently followed
- No systematic variance analysis
- Weak feedback loop between operations and planning

**Documents to Reference:**
- WFM SOPs folder (all docs)
- Process review notes
- Planner interviews

## Perspectives & Angles

### Overall Theme
"Building foundations before automation"

### Key Message for Client
Variance analysis is the critical missing capability — without it, other improvements will be built on sand.

### Strategic Direction to Emphasize
- Consolidate before expanding
- Process before technology
- Foundation before optimization

### Anticipated Client Concerns
- Budget constraints
- Change fatigue from recent initiatives
- Vendor relationship complexity

### Tone
- Constructive, not critical
- Acknowledge their strengths
- Focus on quick wins alongside big changes
