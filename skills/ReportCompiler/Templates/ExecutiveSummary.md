## Writing Guidelines

### Tone
- Objective and professional
- Focus on facts and evidence
- Balance strengths with gaps (don't be purely critical)
- Forward-looking and actionable

### Length
- 1-2 pages maximum
- Each bullet point: 1-2 sentences
- Avoid jargon; write for executive audience

### Key Principles
1. **Lead with the score** — Establish the baseline immediately
2. **Balance positive and negative** — Show you recognize their strengths
3. **Be specific** — Vague findings don't drive action
4. **Prioritize clearly** — Executives need to know what to do first
5. **Quantify when possible** — Numbers are more compelling than adjectives

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- Starting with methodology (save for later)
- Burying the lead (score should be in first paragraph)
- Too many recommendations (5 max for exec summary)
- Unclear prioritization
- Missing the "so what" — always explain impact
