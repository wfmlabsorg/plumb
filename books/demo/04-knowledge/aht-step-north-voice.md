NORTH voice AHT stepped from ~420s to ~587s (1.40x) on 2026-07-13 and has held flat since.

It is a **step with no curve**, which is the discriminator for *structural* rather than
transitional — so it should be staffed for, not buffered against. A learning curve would decay;
this has not moved in six weeks.

Required hours for NORTH voice rose 1.60x across the same window [C] — more than the AHT ratio
alone, because the contact-rate drift ([[contact-rate-drift-north]]) is compounding with it.

**What would change this:** a single week of AHT returning toward 420s would reclassify it as
transitional. It has not happened in six weeks of observation.
